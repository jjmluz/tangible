# Tangible: NFC Orchestrator

## Intro

This is a new project that was just created from scratch. You will have to create all the foundations.
The goal for this app is for it to be an NFC tag media orchestrator called Tangible.
It will work as a way to preserve legacy physical media but bringing it to modern day digital convenience.

## Main use case

- Users stick NFC tags to their old school media (vinyl, CDs, DVDs, etc)
- They use our app to write to the NFC what that media is. Examples:
  - media:movie;name:titanic;year:1997
  - media:album;name:modern_vampires_of_the_city;artist:vampire_weekend;year:2013
- On our app settings, users are able to tell their default:
  - 3rd party app for streaming audio (e.g. Spotify) and video (e.g. Netflix)
  - Player device for audio (e.g. own smartphone, Sonos speakers) and video (e.g. own smartphone, Chromecast TV)
- When users scan a previously written NFC tag, our app opens and automatically starts playing the album or movie on the designated streaming app and player device
